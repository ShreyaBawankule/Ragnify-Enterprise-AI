# Employee Handbook

## Employment Practices

Employees are expected to follow company policies, keep manager communication transparent, and update assigned tasks daily. Working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST with flexibility for approved project needs.

## Attendance

Attendance is tracked through the HRMS. Employees must mark check-in and check-out accurately. Unplanned absence must be reported to the manager and HR before 10:30 AM where possible.

## Performance

Performance reviews consider delivery quality, collaboration, ownership, learning, and reliability. Managers use task data, peer feedback, and project outcomes to support review decisions.
