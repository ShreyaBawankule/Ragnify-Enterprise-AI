# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work from home up to two days per week with manager approval. HR and managers may approve additional remote days for health, travel, or project-specific reasons.

## Expectations

Employees must remain reachable during working hours, attend scheduled meetings, keep task status current, and protect company data on approved devices.

## Security

Work from home requires VPN access, encrypted storage, secure Wi-Fi, and no sharing of company material through personal accounts.
