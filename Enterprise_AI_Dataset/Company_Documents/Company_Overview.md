# Company Overview

TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a Bangalore-based software development and AI solutions company. The company builds enterprise web applications, AI assistants, dashboard systems, and workflow automation tools for internal and client-facing use.

## Departments

- AI Team: builds retrieval augmented generation, model evaluation, and AI assistant workflows.
- Frontend: develops responsive React interfaces for employees and managers.
- Backend: owns APIs, database design, authentication, and deployment services.
- QA: validates features through manual, regression, automation, and release testing.
- HR: manages onboarding, policies, payroll coordination, and employee engagement.

## Operating Model

TechNova works in two-week sprints. Each sprint starts with planning, continues through daily standups and task execution, and ends with review, QA sign-off, and retrospective notes. Managers track delivery through task completion rate, overdue items, productivity, and department performance.
