# Leave Policy

## Leave Types

Employees receive casual leave, sick leave, earned leave, and optional holiday benefits. Leave requests must be submitted through HRMS with manager approval.

## Approval Rules

Planned leave should be requested at least three working days in advance. Sick leave may be regularized after the employee returns to work. Long leave requires project handover notes.

## Restrictions

Leave during critical releases may be deferred when business continuity is at risk, except for medical or emergency reasons.
