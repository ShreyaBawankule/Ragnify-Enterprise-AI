# HR Policy

## Purpose

The HR policy defines employee lifecycle practices at TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd., including recruitment, onboarding, attendance, engagement, performance review, and exit formalities.

## Onboarding

New employees receive offer documentation, system access, policy orientation, and project induction. HR coordinates with managers to assign a mentor for the first thirty days.

## Payroll

Salary is processed monthly after attendance and leave reconciliation. Demo salary values in this dataset are synthetic and must not be treated as real compensation guidance.
